Our Home Care Services

At Ultimate Support Homecare Inc., we provide personalized in-home care services designed to help older adults live safely, comfortably, and confidently at home. Each care plan is tailored to meet individual needs, routines, and preferences.

Personal & Daily Living Care

Hands-on assistance with everyday activities to support comfort, dignity, and independence.

  • Bathing, dressing, and grooming
  • Mobility and transfer assistance
  • Toileting and hygiene support
  • Routine daily activity assistance

Medication Support

Non-medical medication assistance to help ensure routines are followed safely and consistently.

  • Medication reminders
  • Support with medication schedules
  • Observation and reporting of concerns

Homemaker Services

Household support that helps maintain a clean, safe, and comfortable living environment.

  • Light housekeeping
  • Laundry and linen changes
  • Meal preparation and nutrition support
  • Errands and household organization

Companionship & Therapy Support

Emotional support and recovery-focused care that promotes wellness and engagement.

  • Friendly companionship and conversation
  • Encouragement of social engagement
  • Physical, occupational, and speech therapy support
  • Post-hospital or rehabilitation assistance
