Our Services

Ultimate Support Homecare Inc. provides compassionate, reliable in-home care designed to support independence, safety, and quality of life. Our services are tailored to meet the unique needs of each individual and family we serve.

Daily Living Support

Assistance with everyday activities such as bathing, dressing, grooming, mobility, and maintaining a comfortable home environment.

Medication Management

Support with medication reminders to help ensure medications are taken safely, correctly, and on time.

Companionship

Friendly companionship that encourages social engagement, emotional well-being, and meaningful daily interaction.

Therapy Services

Physical, occupational, and speech therapy services provided in the comfort of home to support recovery and mobility.

Homemaker & Personal Care

Our Homemaker and Personal Care services are designed to help older adults remain safe, comfortable, and confident in their own homes. We focus on providing respectful, hands-on support while promoting independence and dignity.

Personal Care Assistance

Support with bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting, and mobility to help maintain comfort, hygiene, and self-confidence.

Meal Preparation & Nutrition

Assistance with preparing nutritious meals and snacks, as well as support with eating routines when needed.

Light Housekeeping

Help with everyday household tasks such as laundry, dishes, tidying, and maintaining a clean, safe living space.

Medication Reminders

Gentle reminders to help ensure medications are taken according to schedule, providing peace of mind for families.

Every personal care plan is customized to meet individual needs, routines, and preferences. Our caregivers provide support with compassion, respect, and a genuine commitment to improving quality of life.
