Our Home Care Services

Ultimate Support Homecare Inc. provides non-medical, in-home support services designed to help individuals live safely and independently in their own homes. Services are personalized based on individual needs, routines, and care goals.

🧍‍♀️ Personal & Daily Living Care

Assistance with essential daily activities to promote dignity, comfort, and personal independence.

  • Bathing, dressing, and grooming assistance
  • Toileting and hygiene support
  • Mobility and transfer assistance
  • Support with daily routines and activities

💊 Medication Support

Non-medical assistance to help individuals follow prescribed medication routines safely.

  • Medication reminders
  • Support with medication schedules
  • Observation and reporting of concerns to families

🏠 Homemaker Services

Household assistance that supports a clean, organized, and safe living environment.

  • Light housekeeping and laundry
  • Meal preparation and nutrition support
  • Errands and grocery assistance
  • Household organization and safety support

🤝 Companionship & Supportive Care

Emotional support and engagement services that promote social interaction, mental wellness, and recovery.

  • Friendly companionship and conversation
  • Encouragement of social activities
  • Support following hospitalization or rehabilitation
  • Therapy support (physical, occupational, speech)
