Ultimate Support Homecare Inc. provides compassionate, dependable in-home care for seniors and adults with disabilities throughout Cincinnati and surrounding communities. Our mission is simple: to help individuals live safely, comfortably, and with dignity in the place they call home.

Who We Serve

Every individual’s needs are different. We take a thoughtful, personalized approach to care — listening closely and creating support plans that truly fit each person’s life, preferences, and goals.

Seniors

Support for aging adults who wish to remain independent while receiving respectful assistance with daily living, safety, and comfort at home.

Adults with Disabilities

Personalized care aligned with Ohio PASSPORT, Medicaid waiver, and Developmental Disabilities (DoDD) programs — focused on independence, routine, and quality of life.

Our In-Home Care Services

Our caregivers provide reliable, compassionate assistance designed to support everyday living — while honoring each individual’s dignity and independence.

Personal & Daily Living Support

Assistance with bathing, dressing, grooming, mobility, and daily routines to help clients feel safe and confident at home.

Homemaker & Household Assistance

Light housekeeping, meal preparation, and home support to maintain a clean, comfortable living environment.

Medication Reminders

Gentle reminders and support to help ensure medications are taken correctly and consistently.

Companionship & Emotional Support

Friendly companionship that promotes connection, emotional well-being, and meaningful daily engagement.

Not sure what care you need?

Choosing home care can feel overwhelming — and you don’t have to figure it out alone. We are happy to listen, answer your questions, and help guide you toward the right level of support.
