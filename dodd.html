Developmental Disabilities Support

Dedicated, person-centered care empowering individuals with developmental disabilities to live safely, independently, and with dignity.

Request Services

Ultimate Support Homecare Inc. provides compassionate in-home support for individuals with developmental disabilities in Cincinnati and surrounding communities. Our services are aligned with Ohio Medicaid and county Developmental Disabilities (DoDD) programs.

We work collaboratively with individuals, families, and service coordinators to deliver respectful, reliable care that supports independence, safety, and quality of life.

DoDD Support Services

Personal & Daily Living Support

Assistance with hygiene, dressing, meal preparation, and routines that promote independence and confidence.

Community Integration

Support with community engagement, social activities, and skill-building to encourage participation and inclusion.

Behavioral & Safety Support

Structured, consistent care to support behavioral goals, safety awareness, and emotional well-being.

Respite for Families

Reliable respite care that provides families and caregivers peace of mind and needed relief.

Who We Serve

Our Developmental Disabilities services support individuals enrolled in Ohio Medicaid waiver programs or receiving services through their local County Board of Developmental Disabilities.

Services are personalized based on individual needs, goals, and care plans.
