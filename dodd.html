Developmental Disabilities Support

Person-centered support through Ohio DoDD programs

Ultimate Support Homecare Inc. provides non-medical, person-centered support services for individuals with developmental disabilities enrolled in Ohio Department of Developmental Disabilities (DoDD) programs.

Support Services May Include:

  • Assistance with daily living skills
  • Community inclusion and engagement
  • Personal care and supervision
  • Skill-building and independence support
  • Behavioral support reinforcement (as outlined in ISP)

Services are delivered according to each individual’s Individual Service Plan (ISP) and applicable Medicaid waiver requirements.
