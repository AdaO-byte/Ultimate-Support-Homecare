Care shaped around the individual

We provide in-home support for people with developmental disabilities in Cincinnati and surrounding communities, aligned with Ohio Medicaid and county DoDD programs.

We work with individuals, families, and service coordinators to deliver respectful, reliable care that supports independence, safety, and quality of life.

DoDD support services

Personal and daily living support

Hygiene, dressing, meal preparation, and routines that build confidence.

Community integration

Community engagement, social activities, and skill-building.

Behavioral and safety support

Consistent care that supports behavioral goals and safety awareness.

Respite for families

Reliable relief and peace of mind for families and caregivers.

Who we serve

We support people enrolled in Ohio Medicaid waiver programs or receiving services through their County Board of Developmental Disabilities. Services are personalized to each person’s needs, goals, and care plan.
