PASSPORT Waiver Home Care

Compassionate, reliable in-home care supporting seniors who wish to remain safe, independent, and comfortable in their own homes.

Request Services

Ultimate Support Homecare Inc. provides high-quality in-home care services for seniors enrolled in Ohio’s PASSPORT Medicaid waiver program throughout Cincinnati and surrounding communities.

The PASSPORT program is designed to help older adults remain in their homes rather than transitioning to nursing facilities. Our team works closely with clients, families, and care managers to deliver dependable, person-centered support that promotes dignity, safety, and independence.

PASSPORT Covered Services

Personal Care Assistance

Support with bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, and mobility to help seniors maintain comfort and personal dignity.

Homemaker Services

Light housekeeping, laundry, meal preparation, and home organization to ensure a clean, safe living environment.

Medication Reminders

Gentle reminders to support medication adherence and daily routines, promoting overall health and wellness.

Companionship & Supervision

Meaningful companionship, safety monitoring, and social engagement to reduce isolation and support emotional well-being.

Who We Serve

Our PASSPORT services support seniors aged 60 and older who are eligible for Ohio Medicaid and enrolled in the PASSPORT waiver program through their local Area Agency on Aging.

Care plans are tailored to each individual’s needs, preferences, and level of independence, ensuring support that fits seamlessly into daily life.
