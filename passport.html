Support through Ohio’s PASSPORT program

We provide in-home care for seniors enrolled in Ohio’s PASSPORT Medicaid waiver program throughout Cincinnati and surrounding communities.

PASSPORT helps older adults remain at home rather than move to a nursing facility. We work closely with clients, families, and care managers to deliver person-centered support.

PASSPORT covered services

Personal care assistance

Bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, and mobility support.

Homemaker services

Light housekeeping, laundry, meal preparation, and organization.

Medication reminders

Reminders that support daily routines and health.

Companionship and supervision

Safety monitoring and social engagement to reduce isolation.

Who we serve

Our PASSPORT services support adults aged 60 and older who are eligible for Ohio Medicaid and enrolled in PASSPORT through their local Area Agency on Aging. Care plans fit each person’s needs, preferences, and level of independence.
