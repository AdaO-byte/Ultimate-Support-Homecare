Caregiver position

We are seeking compassionate, dependable caregivers to support seniors and adults with disabilities across Cincinnati and surrounding areas.

Responsibilities

  • Assist with personal care, mobility, and daily living
  • Provide companionship and emotional support
  • Support clients enrolled in PASSPORT and DoDD programs
  • Maintain a safe, respectful, dignified care environment

Qualifications

  • At least 18 years old
  • Legally authorized to work in the U.S.
  • Dependable transportation required
  • Home care experience highly preferred
  • Ability to pass required background checks

Specializations

Opportunities are available to support people with developmental disabilities through Ohio DoDD programs. Related training is a plus.
