Caregiver Position

Ultimate Support Homecare Inc. is seeking compassionate, dependable caregivers to support seniors and adults with disabilities throughout Cincinnati and surrounding areas.

Responsibilities

  • Assist with personal care, mobility, and daily living activities
  • Provide companionship and emotional support
  • Support clients enrolled in PASSPORT and DoDD programs
  • Maintain a safe, respectful, and dignified care environment

Qualifications

  • Must be at least 18 years old
  • Legally authorized to work in the U.S.
  • Dependable transportation is required
  • Experience in home care or caregiving is highly preferred
  • Ability to pass required background checks

Specializations

Opportunities are available to support individuals with developmental disabilities through Ohio DoDD programs. Training and experience in this area are a plus.
