About Ultimate Support Homecare Inc.

Compassionate, dependable in-home care rooted in dignity, respect, and trust.

Ultimate Support Homecare Inc. was founded with one clear mission: to provide high-quality, personalized in-home care that allows individuals to remain safe, comfortable, and independent in their own homes.

Ultimate Support Homecare Inc. provides compassionate, reliable in-home care for seniors and adults with disabilities in Cincinnati, Ohio. Our services include personal care, homemaker assistance, medication reminders, companionship, and daily living support.

We proudly support individuals enrolled in Ohio Medicaid programs, including PASSPORT waivers and Developmental Disabilities (DoDD) services. Our goal is to help clients live safely, comfortably, and independently while giving families peace of mind.

Serving Cincinnati and surrounding communities with dignity, respect, and personalized care.

Who We Serve

We proudly serve seniors and adults with physical, developmental, or cognitive disabilities who need support with daily living activities. Our care plans are customized to meet each client’s unique needs, preferences, and goals.

Our Commitment

We believe that quality care goes beyond tasks — it’s about building trust, fostering independence, and treating every individual with compassion and respect. Our caregivers are carefully selected, trained, and supported to deliver consistent, dependable care.

Why Families Choose Us

  • Locally owned and community focused
  • Experienced and compassionate caregivers
  • Ohio Medicaid, PASSPORT & DoDD program support
  • Personalized care plans
  • Clear communication and family peace of mind
