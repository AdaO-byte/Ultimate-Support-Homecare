About Ultimate Support Homecare Inc.

Compassionate, dependable in-home care rooted in dignity, respect, and trust.

Ultimate Support Homecare Inc. was founded with a simple but meaningful purpose: to help people receive the care they need while remaining safe, comfortable, and at home — where they belong.

For nearly two decades, we’ve been honored to provide compassionate, dependable in-home care for seniors and adults with disabilities throughout Cincinnati, Ohio and surrounding areas. We understand that inviting someone into your home is a deeply personal decision, and we don’t take that trust lightly. Our caregivers focus not just on daily tasks, but on building genuine relationships rooted in respect and kindness.

Our services include personal care, homemaker assistance, medication reminders, companionship, and daily living support — all delivered with attention to each individual’s routine, preferences, and comfort.

We proudly support individuals enrolled in Ohio Medicaid programs, including PASSPORT waivers and Developmental Disabilities (DoDD) services. Whether care is short-term or ongoing, our goal is always the same: to help clients live safely and independently while giving families confidence and peace of mind.

Serving Cincinnati and surrounding communities with dignity, compassion, and personalized care has been our mission since day one — and it continues to guide everything we do.

Who We Serve

We proudly serve seniors and adults with physical, developmental, or cognitive disabilities who need support with daily living activities. Every individual’s situation is unique, which is why we take the time to listen, understand preferences, and create care plans that reflect personal goals, routines, and comfort levels.

Our Commitment

At Ultimate Support Homecare, we believe quality care is about more than completing tasks — it’s about trust, consistency, and treating people with genuine compassion. Our caregivers are carefully selected, and supported to ensure dependable care and meaningful connections with the individuals and families we serve.

Why Families Choose Us

  • Locally owned and community focused
  • Compassionate, reliable, and experienced caregivers
  • Support for Ohio Medicaid, PASSPORT, and DoDD programs
  • Personalized care plans tailored to each individual
  • Clear communication and peace of mind for families
