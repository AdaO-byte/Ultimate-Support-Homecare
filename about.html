Our story

Ultimate Support Homecare Inc. was founded on a simple belief: people deserve care that honors their independence, comfort, and dignity in the place they call home.

We provide in-home care for seniors and adults with disabilities across Cincinnati and surrounding communities, including personal care, homemaker help, medication reminders, and companionship.

We proudly support people enrolled in Ohio Medicaid programs, including PASSPORT and Developmental Disabilities (DoDD) services.

Who we serve

We serve seniors and adults with physical, developmental, or cognitive disabilities who need help with daily living. We take time to listen and build care plans around personal goals, routines, and comfort.

Our commitment

Quality care is more than completing tasks. It means building trust, supporting independence, and showing up with compassion every day. Our caregivers are carefully selected and supported.

Why families choose us

  • Locally owned and community focused
  • Compassionate, reliable, experienced caregivers
  • Support for Ohio Medicaid, PASSPORT, and DoDD programs
  • Personalized care plans for each individual
  • Clear communication and peace of mind for families
