About Ultimate Support Homecare Inc.

Compassionate, dependable in-home care rooted in dignity, respect, and trust.

Ultimate Support Homecare Inc. was founded with a simple belief but meaningful purpose: people deserve care that honors their independence, comfort, and dignity — right in the place they call home.

We provide compassionate, reliable in-home care for seniors and adults with disabilities throughout Cincinnati and surrounding communities. Our services include personal care, homemaker assistance, medication reminders, companionship, and daily living support — all delivered with consistency, respect, and genuine human connection.

We proudly support individuals enrolled in Ohio Medicaid programs, including PASSPORT waivers and Developmental Disabilities (DoDD) services. Our goal is to help clients live safely and comfortably at home while giving families confidence and peace of mind.

Who We Serve

We serve seniors and adults with physical, developmental, or cognitive disabilities who need support with daily living activities. Every individual’s situation is unique, which is why we take the time to listen, understand preferences, and create care plans that reflect personal goals, routines, and comfort levels.

Our Commitment

Quality care is about more than completing tasks. It’s about building trust, fostering independence, and showing up with compassion every day. Our caregivers are carefully selected, and supported to provide dependable care that families can truly rely on.

Why Families Choose Us

  • Locally owned and community focused
  • Compassionate, reliable, and experienced caregivers
  • Support for Ohio Medicaid, PASSPORT, and DoDD programs
  • Personalized care plans tailored to each individual
  • Clear communication and peace of mind for families
